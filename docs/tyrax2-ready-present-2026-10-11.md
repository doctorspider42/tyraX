## Decision

Protected old-job publication is possible, but this per-StaPip/recording-start
intervention is not the missing large gain. Keep private OFF; do not rescue it
with cache/poll tuning or infer that all EE preparation is efficient. Normal
submission and all DMA/FINISH fences remain unchanged in production.

Next inspect existing A13 integrated VU paint qualification under the newly
qualified THREE-buffer context, not label a known offload as a new discovery.
Its older physical first order saves about1ms but reverse/default-off image cost
and new combined ABI/ownership/raster gates remain open. Test the combination,
never sum unrelated measurements or assume it gives60. Normal-production
integration/boot is still required after any successful private combination.
