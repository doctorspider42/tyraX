# Runtime witness scope

Root supplied completed/stopped emulator records for vehicle Players and animated Players/All. The archive preserves their launch, controller and capture records and final logs; it does not reinterpret model watch snapshots as authoritative player movement.

Root's reviewed vehicle captures show normal driven-car and exited-player output. Logs record vehicle movement and VEHexit. This demonstrates exercised driving/exit and normal appearance, not exact shader arithmetic, all lamps, traffic classes or every secondary view.

In the animated Players fixture, root reviewed captures where both split player cats receive strong red/pink live point light while ordinary animated cats remain gray. Player1-left and Player2-right controller routes produce independently changed views/positions, and world cats show different animated poses across captures. The All counterpart capture shows players, ordinary cats and wall receiving red/pink light. This is positive qualitative receiver-policy evidence across two compiled variants. It is not a same-pose pixel oracle, byte-identical comparison, exact animation cursor trace or PS2 performance measurement. Initial menu-only captures are not activation witnesses.

Hardware Players and showcase output are not yet included as completed qualification in this snapshot. Root reports fresh hardware startup and normal capture, but the active client log is withheld until stopped. Native compilation for showcase is represented separately; it cannot establish its rendered frame rate or public option image behavior.

The command encoder binds the actual pad/watch protocol. It is controller/tool evidence, not a shader ownership witness by itself. Target ABI/VU images and further runtime records remain pending or independently scoped by their own proof documents.
