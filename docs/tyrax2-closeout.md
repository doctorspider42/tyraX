# TyraX2 closeout qualification

The production renderer foundation, default selection, flow-graph integration
and public dynamic-light receiver option are implemented. Stable full-quality
night 60 fps and current public-option hardware pricing remain open. This
2026-10-06 qualification does not promote another speculative renderer change.

The receiver/geometry fixture uses the same ELF for its controls. It retains
the ordinary loop/presentation clocks, with FrameProfile=0 and HardwareTrace=0,
and adds no completion fence. Five 64-loop chunks cover each warmed 320-loop
window; the On sampler records 128 raw loops within it. Exports and cold
context witnesses sit outside those windows. The sample/chunk arrays are
6144/420 bytes; common compiled footprint remains unpriced.

Players retains scene light pools and other effects. Hiding the eleven authored
night boxes is a separate appearance reduction, not equivalent rendering or
an additive saving inferred from historical experiments. The additional
garage blade copy remains in both arms. World time, audio and intentional lamp
flicker can drift; fixed camera/midnight selection does not freeze every state.

The motion fixtures are one-arm Players correctness/cadence routes, not matched
A/B savings. Phase state carries forward. Cold endpoint positions do not prove
the entire trajectory, and the largest observed chunk mean is not a worst
individual frame or p99. Showcase counts successful teleport branches on both
portals; controller input alone is insufficient evidence of a crossing.

## Apparatus corrections

Every nonzero `TYRA_FRAME_PROFILE`, including level 2, enables the fairness
`sync.align3D()` drain in `RendererCore::endFrame`. Level 2 removes the packet
walker but does not measure ordinary asynchronous TyraX2 overlap. Earlier
level-2 records remain diagnostic records; matching HUD SCENE cannot certify
transparent instrumentation.

Devkit-off also leaves authored VEH/VEHCONTACT telemetry and Vif1Queue arena
reports/RAM probes active. Initial v3 trials completed their protocols but
remain excluded from quiet timing. Private v4 removes those healthy report
blocks and suppresses healthy TYRA_LOG while retaining warnings, errors,
traps and assertions. Actual native source/object/archive/ELF bindings were
checked. This private apparatus does not change production logging behavior.

PCSX2 establishes source/control activation and rendering correctness; its
elapsed numbers do not price PS2. Audio is not qualified. Neither a screenshot
FPS counter nor flip-return timestamps prove physical TV scanout or input
latency. Current public policy controls also carry common selector/layout
cost: their relative All/Players delta cannot price the pristine default path.

## Hardware status

The attempted fresh hardware start repeatedly reported `freepad: DMA Busy`
without loadelf or a game-start marker. It is rejected as **no game start**,
not a renderer failure or timing sample. The owned client was stopped, and
physical power-cycle/ps2link readiness is pending. No new physical performance
numbers or stable-night-60 acceptance are reported here.

The [earlier physical day/night census](tyrax2-claude-trials.md) was approximately 14.5–14.6 ms
versus 18.3–18.4 ms of non-pacing work. These historical values describe their
own apparatus; do not subtract private receiver or geometry gains from them
to predict the new ELF. The 60 Hz budget is approximately 16.68 ms, and missing
the presentation deadline can produce a roughly 33.37 ms return period.

The [frozen evidence archive](tyrax2-closeout-2026-10-06/README.md) retains
selected raw records, native/source proofs, scripts and screenshots. It excludes
ELF/SDK/assets while retaining their provenance hashes. No hardware timing result
is implied by its inventory.
