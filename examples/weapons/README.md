# weapons example

A shooting range: three weapons, five crates that come apart, two turrets
that shoot back, and a HUD wired out of two pure text nodes — the
[Weapons and combat](../../docs/weapons.md) feature end to end.

Open `weapons.tyra` in the editor and Build & Run (`F5`), or build headless:
`tyrax-editor.exe --build <this folder> --run`.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| **R1** | Fire / swing |
| **Triangle** | Reload |
| **L1** | Next weapon (Pistol → Shotgun → Machete → …) |
| **Start** | Respawn after you die |

Left stick walks, right stick looks; with keyboard & mouse on, the mouse's
left button also fires.

## What to do

You start at the south edge with a **Pistol**, a **Shotgun** and a
**Machete**.

- **Shoot the crates.** They have 60 health and *Death = Knock over*, so they
  topple under the physics simulation instead of vanishing. Their *Hit
  effect* is **Dust**, which overrides whatever the weapon would have
  thrown — the same shot sparks off the wall and puffs off a crate.
- **Cycle to the Shotgun** (L1). Eight pellets, 6° spread and a brutal 0.75
  falloff over 30 units: devastating against a crate you are standing next
  to, nearly useless across the range. Watch the recoil kick and settle.
- **Take the Machete** and walk into a crate. The swing lands *mid-arc*, not
  on the button press, and hits everything damageable inside a 60° cone.
- **The two turrets** at the back have an *Auto-fire range* of 28 units and a
  75° vision cone, and they need terrain line of sight — so the **grey wall**
  in front of them is real cover. Step out and your HP counter starts
  dropping; duck back and it stops. They have 120 health each; the Pistol
  takes about six hits.
- **Die on purpose** to see the scripted game-over: the Player object's own
  Death action is ignored by the runtime, so the graph draws
  "YOU ARE DEAD - PRESS START" from an **On Killed** trigger and Start heals,
  teleports and re-ammoes you.

## How it is wired

Everything below is in the editor, no C++:

- **Tools > Weapon Editor** — the three definitions. Their viewmodels
  (`vm-pistol`, `vm-shotgun`, `vm-machete`) are ordinary Model objects in the
  scene, generated by *Viewmodel > Generate a model*: 48–140 triangles each,
  procedural, licence-free. The same tab previews each of them from the
  player's eye, animation and all, so their offsets can be judged without a
  build.
- **Properties > Combat** on the crates and turrets — Damageable, health,
  death action, hit effect; the turrets additionally carry a Pistol and an
  auto-fire range.
- The **player's flow graph** — `Get Ammo As Text` → `Display Text` for
  "AMMO 12 / 60", `Get Health As Text` → `Display Text` for "HP", and the
  `On Killed` → `On Button (Start)` → `Apply Damage (set)` + `Spawn Player At`
  + `Set Ammo` respawn chain. There is no bespoke weapon HUD in the engine;
  this is it.

The player is marked **Damageable** — without that the turrets would fire and
nothing would happen.
